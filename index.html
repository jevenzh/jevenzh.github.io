I'm a senior research engineer at Neurogin, leading the data and algorithm team. My work spans in-house EEG data collection, digital signal processing (DSP), machine learning model training, and production-grade deployment on custom EEG hardware.

I'm particularly fascinated by time-series data, reinforcement learning, and exploring how the human brain processes information. I often share novel ideas and recent papers on Reddit — especially in communities like r/MachineLearning, r/bioinformatics, and r/ArtificialIntelligence.

A strong believer in connectionism, I find biologically inspired approaches to AGI deeply compelling. One of my favorite thinkers is Lex Fridman (his podcast is a gem). I'm especially intrigued by HTM (Hierarchical Temporal Memory) as a theoretical framework for intelligence.

Conversations are very welcome from the worldwide (English or Chinese), just leave comments under my blog posts or through email jeven-z@hotmail.com.
