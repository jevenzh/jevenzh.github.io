Among all the approaches that dedicated to achieving AGI, my focus is on the multi-modal disentangled representation learning. I will elaborate my perspective in the following section based on three papers: the consciousness prior, contrastive predictive coding (CPC) and hierarchical representation learning (HRL).

Firstly, Yoshua Bengio has proposed a theory of learning representation based on a more biological/psychological plausible prior, which is called the consciousness prior. It started by asking what’s wrong with the current deep learning, in which we learn the representation of our data/observations using deep neural networks? As established in many state-of-the-art works in many AI subfields, current deep neural networks are able to learn effective "high-level" representations for classification or reconstruction (generative) tasks. However, such distributed dense representations make neural networks hard to understand, like a "black box", therefore it is crucial to disentangle abstract factors from each other in the distributed representation of high-level features. The consciousness prior proposes a methodology to implement such disentanglement by hypothesizing the representation in a conscious state should be of low capacity, for instance, a low-dimensional thought vector. All predictions or reconstruction tasks should be conducted in this space, and those lower stream tasks are supposed to be learned in a much more efficient manner in the low-dimensional space.

Moreover, I would like to look into the question of how to implement such prior. CPC and hierarchical reinforcement learning (HRL), in my opinion, are exactly two of the all possible approaches. They are two main shared key ideas in these two works that match the assumption of the consciousness prior, one is the higher-level abstraction, another is the indirect objective - the mutual information. Note that such "off-task" objective, i.e. the use of mutual information as part of the objective, is very similar to the verifier networks in the consciousness prior theory, it indicates correlations of observations in different time step (temporal dimension), which lies in the very core of the world of coherence. On the other hand, what is the purpose of the higher-level goal policy G in HRL and the much more compact context embedding C in CPC? I claim that they are both extracting a higher-level of abstraction, but they tried to disentangle those abstract factors in different ways. Particularly, CPC disentangles them by predicting the "contrastive coding", associating the context embedding to the future lower-level latent space, while the HRL interacts higher-level goal space with the lower-level action space, in order to disentangle abstract factors in the goal space. Although the G in HRL is directly derived from observation space and the C in CPC is extracted from the latent/encoding space, they are essentially doing the same thing. (it is a quite strong claim, but I believe it holds)

Finally, it is always interesting to ask what can we do in the future. I think we should realize that we are leaving the era of pattern recognition and perception, intelligence must be built upon perception - we need to learn representations that can efficiently encoding concepts, relations and so on. A connection between high abstraction space and the human symbolic/language space is inevitable as long as we aim to a human-centered AGI.
