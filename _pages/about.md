I am currently a senior research engineer at Neurogin, where I lead the data & algorithm team in the meanwhile. This includes in-house data labeling, DSP (digital signal processing) algorithm development, machine learning model training, the science of how EEG (electroencephalogram) work, and deployment in production running on our custom EEG aquisition hardware. Among many interesting topics, I am particularly fond of time-series data processing, reinforcement learning, and exploring how the human brain works. I enjoy discusing about noval ideas or recent papers of these topics on Reddit, my favorite reddits includes [r/MachineLearning](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/), [r/bioinformatics](https://www.reddit.com/r/bioinformatics/) and [r/ArtificialInteligence](https://www.reddit.com/r/ArtificialInteligence).

By the way, I am a big fun of the connectionism, that is biology-inspired way of approching artificial general intellegence. One of my favorite AI preachers is Lex Fridman, his [podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/cn/podcast/lex-fridman-podcast/id1434243584) is very inspiring. The [HTM](https://numenta.com/blog/2019/10/24/machine-learning-guide-to-htm) (Hierachical Temporal Memory) theory is so far my favorite learning/intellegence theory on the planet earth.
